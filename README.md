# Learning-Journal-Blog-Entry-1
### CORE REFLECTION

They call me Coach J, I'm a endurance sports coach, event organizer, and the founder of Eightensity Active Sport in Digos City, Davao del Sur. I recognized for coaching runners of all abilities and for organizing road running, trail running, and multisport events in the region.

### COACH J

#### I am UESCA (United Endurance Sports Coaching Academy) Certified Running Coach with experience coaching:

1. Road running (5K to marathon)
2. Trail running
3. Ultrarunning
4. Triathlon
5. Strength and conditioning
6. Injury prevention
7. Running form analysis

My coaching philosophy centers on the belief that "Anyone Can Run." I emphasizes evidence-based training, personalized coaching, proper running mechanics, and long-term athlete development rather than simply chasing race results.

### As a Founder of Eightensity Active Sport

#### Eightensity Active Sport is a Digos City-based sports organization focused on:

1. Organizing road races, trail races, and multisport events
2. Running coaching and athlete development
3. Building an active lifestyle community
4. Supporting beginners through experienced athletes
5. Promoting health, tourism, and community engagement through sports

Public project documents describe Eightensity as an organization that has organized numerous endurance events and aims to make quality coaching and professionally managed races more accessible in Davao del Sur.

#### In behalf of my team, Eightensity Active Sport, we promote the idea that endurance sports should be:

1. Accessible to everyone
2. Safe and professionally organized
3. Educational as well as competitive
4. Community-driven
5. A tool for improving physical and mental well-being

## My Areas of Work

### I, as a coach is involved in:

1. Personal and group running coaching
2. Race directing and technical operations

Overall, I/we built Eightensity Active Sport around the goal of helping people—from first-time runners to competitive athletes—develop confidence, improve performance, and enjoy the lifelong benefits of running while raising the standard of endurance events in the Davao Region.
