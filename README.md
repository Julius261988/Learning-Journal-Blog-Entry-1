## Learning Journal – Blog Entry 1
### ABOUT ME

I am known as Coach J, an endurance sports coach, event organizer, and the founder of Eightensity Active Sport based in Digos City, Davao del Sur. I am dedicated to coaching runners of all levels and organizing professionally managed road running, trail running, and multisport events throughout the region.

#### Coach J

I am a UESCA (United Endurance Sports Coaching Academy) Certified Running Coach with experience in coaching:

1. Road running (5K to marathon)
2. Trail running
3. Ultrarunning
4. Triathlon
5. Strength and conditioning
6. Injury prevention
7. Running form analysis

My coaching philosophy is built on the belief that "Anyone Can Run." I emphasize evidence-based training, personalized coaching, proper running mechanics, and long-term athlete development rather than simply pursuing race results.

#### Founder of Eightensity Active Sport

Eightensity Active Sport is a sports organization based in Digos City, Davao del Sur, dedicated to:

1. Organizing road races, trail races, and multisport events
2. Providing running coaching and athlete development programs
3. Building an active and supportive sports community
4. Guiding beginners while helping experienced athletes reach their full potential
5. Promoting health, tourism, and community engagement through sports

Our organization has successfully organized numerous endurance events and is committed to making quality coaching and professionally managed races more accessible throughout Davao del Sur and the surrounding communities.

#### On behalf of the Eightensity Active Sport team, we believe endurance sports should be:

1. Accessible to everyone
2. Safe and professionally organized
3. Educational as well as competitive
4. Community-driven
5. A powerful tool for improving physical and mental well-being

## WHY I JOINED THE BOOTCAMP

The coaching industry in my area has become highly competitive, and many people now call themselves coaches even without formal training or certification. As a UESCA Certified Running Coach, I believe that continuous learning is essential to staying relevant and providing quality coaching. This motivated me to explore a broader field beyond coaching by learning technology, artificial intelligence (AI), and digital marketing.

I joined this bootcamp because I want to adapt to the changing landscape of coaching and business. Today, success is not only about having expertise in a specific field but also about knowing how to leverage technology to reach more people, communicate effectively, and deliver better services. I believe these skills will help me grow both professionally and as an entrepreneur.

Another reason I joined is to learn how AI can improve my productivity. By understanding programming and AI tools, I can automate repetitive tasks, manage athlete and event data more efficiently, and create marketing materials without relying heavily on additional staff. This will help me maximize my time, reduce operational costs, and allow me to focus on more important aspects of coaching and business development.

Ultimately, my goal is to combine my experience in endurance sports with modern technology to strengthen Eightensity Active Sport. I want to build a more innovative, efficient, and sustainable organization that provides high-quality coaching, professionally organized events, and greater value to the running community while continuing to grow with the ever-evolving digital world.

## WHAT INTEREST ME THE MOST

What interests me the most is how platforms like Facebook, Strava, and Garmin have transformed the way people connect, share information, and build communities. They are more than just applications—they are powerful digital ecosystems that help individuals and businesses reach a wider audience, track progress, and create meaningful engagement. Inspired by these platforms, I want to learn how to develop websites, applications, and business systems that can help entrepreneurs market their products, streamline their operations, and grow their businesses online. Although these technologies have been available for years, many local businesses in the Philippines are still struggle to adopt them because they find digital tools overwhelming or are unsure how to integrate them into their daily operations.

This bootcamp provides the opportunity to bridge that gap. By learning programming, artificial intelligence, and data-driven technologies, I hope to create practical and user-friendly digital solutions tailored to the needs of Filipino entrepreneurs. My goal is to make technology more accessible so that businesses can confidently embrace digital transformation, improve efficiency, reduce costs, and remain competitive in today's rapidly evolving digital economy.

## WHAT WILL I BUILD AFTER 20 WEEKS

Beyond business, I am passionate about using technology to strengthen the running community and promote healthier lifestyles. One of my long-term goals is to develop a local running application that connects runners within each region, allowing them to track their progress, discover nearby running routes and events, find training partners, join local clubs, and motivate one another throughout their fitness journey. I envision an application that is designed specifically for Filipino runners, making it easier for beginners and experienced athletes alike to become part of a supportive and active community.

While this idea has the potential to become a sustainable business, my greatest motivation is to create a positive social impact rather than simply generate profit. I want to use technology to encourage more people to embrace an active lifestyle, improve their physical and mental well-being, and build meaningful connections through sports. By combining my experience as a running coach with the knowledge I gain from this bootcamp, I hope to create digital solutions that not only solve real-world problems but also inspire stronger, healthier, and more connected communities across the Philippines.

## WHAT DID I LEARN (WEEK 1)

During the first week of the bootcamp, I discovered that there are many tools and technologies available that can help me learn more efficiently and become more productive throughout the program. I also gained a better understanding of how data analysis plays a fundamental role in artificial intelligence. AI works by processing and learning from large amounts of data, identifying patterns, and using that information to generate insights or assist in solving similar problems in the future. This enables AI to reduce the time needed to analyze information and make informed decisions.

One of the biggest lessons I learned is that AI is not designed to replace people but to enhance the way we work. It can automate repetitive tasks, organize information, and provide valuable insights, allowing us to focus on more creative, strategic, and meaningful work. I believe that the people who will benefit the most from AI are those who are willing to learn, adapt, and use it as a tool to improve their skills and productivity rather than fear it.
